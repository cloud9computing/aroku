Aroku

Everything the app can do for us, so far

A running list of what's live in Aroku — no update to install, it's already there next time you open it.

Last updated 3 September 2026 Works the same on everyone's phone
📲

Add it to your home screen

Aroku installs like a real app — full-screen, its own icon, no App Store needed.

iPhone — tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android — tap the menu (⋮), then "Install app".
💬

Talk to Aroku

  • Ask it anything about the family's records, medicines, or visits — it answers from what's actually saved, and shows you which record it got the answer from.
  • Tell it something to pass along to a doctor ("tell Dr. Iyer about the dizziness") — it drafts a note that shows up automatically on that doctor's next visit.
  • Add a medicine or an appointment just by describing it in plain language — it always asks you to confirm before saving anything.
📄

Records

  • Scan or upload a lab report, prescription, or discharge summary — it reads out the values, diagnoses, and medicines for you.
  • Nothing is trusted blindly: every extracted value sits there until someone taps Verify.
  • Medicines spotted in a scan show up right on the record with a one-tap Add to your medicines.
  • Records file themselves into the right month automatically, and a Merge similar test names tool keeps trend charts accurate when the same test gets labelled differently across reports.
💊

Medicines

  • The full active list, grouped by time of day, so today's doses are obvious at a glance.
  • Medicines with a stated end date flag themselves once the course is over.
  • A dedicated Capture Prescription option scans a prescription straight into the list.
🗓️

Visits

  • Upcoming and past appointments live on one timeline.
  • Before an appointment, it drafts questions to ask — the point is connecting dots between specialists who never talk to each other ("your cardiologist started X — does that need to pause before today's procedure?").
  • After the appointment, mark it complete with a recap: upload the recording (it transcribes, detects the language, and translates to English if it wasn't in English), scan a written summary, or just type the key takeaways.
  • Share a pre-visit brief straight to WhatsApp, or print a one-page sheet to hand a specialist.
🩺

Doctors

  • Every doctor's contact details in one place — tap Call or Directions right from the card.
  • Human doctors and pets' vets are kept separate, so switching to a pet's profile only shows their vets.
Directions works best if you paste in a Google Maps link — a typed address still works, it just searches instead.
🐾

Works for pets too

  • Records, medicines, visits, and doctors all work exactly the same way for a dog, cat, or other pet — not a separate, cut-down version.

That's everything so far · more on the way.
