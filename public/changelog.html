Aroku

Your specialists don't talk to each other. Let Aroku talk to them.

Ever wished all the various specialist doctors you consult were on a group chat with you, with full shared context about your parent/pet, instead of each one operating in their own silos? Aroku is that shared context you can walk into every doctor consultation with.

Talk to Aroku

  • Ask it anything about the family's records, medicines, or visits — it answers only from what's actually saved, with a link back to the source, and draws an actual trend chart when there's history to show ("how has his HbA1c been?").
  • Add a medicine — one, or a whole list at once — just by describing it in plain language.
  • Change a dose or timing on a medicine already on the list ("increase metformin to 1000mg").
  • Stop a medicineNew — entirely, or just one dose while the rest continues ("stop the evening dose of metformin, keep the morning one"). It always shows you the exact medicine it means and waits for a tap before touching anything — never a silent guess.
  • Book an appointment just by describing it ("appointment with Dr Nair next Thursday at 4:30").
  • Schedule a not-yet-done testNew as a reminder ("schedule an HbA1c test for next Monday") — it shows up as a card in Records on that date.
  • Tell it something to pass along to a doctor ("tell Dr. Iyer about the dizziness") — it drafts a note that shows up automatically on that doctor's next visit.
  • Nothing above ever saves itself — every draft shows up for a confirm-or-cancel tap first.

Records

  • Scan or upload a lab report, prescription, or discharge summary — it reads out the values, diagnoses, and medicines for you, and now captures every page of a multi-page report in one go.
  • Nothing is trusted blindly: every extracted value sits there until someone taps Verify.
  • Medicines spotted in a scan show up right on the record with a one-tap Add to your medicines.
  • Records file themselves into the right month automatically, and a Merge similar test names tool keeps trend charts accurate when the same test gets labelled differently across reports.
  • Scheduled test remindersNew — ask Aroku to schedule a test and a reminder card sits right in the timeline on its due date. Attach the report when it's ready, mark it done by hand, reschedule it, or remove it — and it flags itself if the date's passed with nothing done yet.
  • The toolbar's tidier now — Merge similar test names and Find medicines in past scans live behind a small ⋯ menu instead of crowding the top of the screen.

Visits

  • Upcoming and past appointments live on one timeline.
  • Before an appointment, it drafts questions to ask — the point is connecting dots between specialists who never talk to each other ("your cardiologist started X — does that need to pause before today's procedure?").
  • After the appointment, mark it complete with a recap: upload the recording (it transcribes, detects the language, and translates to English if it wasn't in English), scan a written summary, or just type the key takeaways.
  • A saved recap can now be editedNew afterwards, if something needs correcting or adding.
  • An upcoming visit's Edit detailsNew now covers everything — doctor, specialty, location, reason, date and time — not just the date, plus a one-tap Mark completed.
  • A visit whose date has passed without being marked complete now flags itself in ochre, right on the timeline, so it doesn't get quietly forgotten.
  • Capture AppointmentNew — scan a consultation sheet from the Visits tab, and if it mentions a follow-up date, Aroku offers to add it straight to your Visits.
  • Share a pre-visit brief straight to WhatsApp, or print a one-page sheet to hand a specialist.

Doctors

  • Every doctor's contact details in one place — tap Call or Directions right from the card.
  • Human doctors and pets' vets are kept separate, so switching to a pet's profile only shows their vets.
  • Capture Doctor DetailsNew — scan a visiting card or a prescription pad header from the Doctors tab, and it pulls out the name, specialty, clinic, phone, and address for you to confirm.
Directions works best if you paste in a Google Maps link — a typed address still works, it just searches instead.
🔔

Reminders

  • Heads-up notificationsNew — 7, 3, and 1 day before an upcoming visit or a scheduled test, a reminder shows up under the bell at the top of the app.
  • Tap one to jump straight to the visit or the test that needs attention.
🐾

Works for pets too

  • Records, medicines, visits, and doctors all work exactly the same way for a dog, cat, or other pet — not a separate, cut-down version.
  • Even Talk to Aroku adapts — ask it to schedule a deworming or a vet visit the same way you'd ask about a person's test or appointment.
